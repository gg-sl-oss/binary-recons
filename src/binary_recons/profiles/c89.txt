C89 SOURCE PROFILE

- Produce C accepted by a C89-era compiler. Do not use declarations in for-loop
  initializers, mixed declarations and statements within one block, compound
  literals, designated initializers, variable-length arrays, or other C99+
  syntax.
- Put each meaningful local declaration before statements in its containing
  block. Preserve declaration order when assembly exposes stack-slot or
  register-allocation differences.
- Use the target project's declared integer and pointer types. Do not silently
  replace narrower types with int when operand width or extension instructions
  distinguish them.
